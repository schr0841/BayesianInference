# Try these with --show-tools

- Compare Bayesian and frequentist slope estimates for our sample data. Explain why their intervals differ.
- Fit the Bayesian model with only five observations. Compare prior SD 0.1 and 10 and explain sensitivity.
- Predict at x=1 and x=5. Distinguish uncertainty in a mean from uncertainty in a new observation and discuss extrapolation.
- What would change if the noise variance were unknown?
- Can a p-value tell me the probability that the null hypothesis is true?

The first three should generate real fitting-tool calls. The last two can be
conceptual answers. The initial version has no multi-turn memory: each invocation
is a fresh run, so include necessary context in each question.
